Summary

PhD Candidate at Hanyang University working on two threads. Interpretability & Safety: white-box use of model internals — attention, entropy, confidence dynamics — to detect hallucination, assess reasoning quality, and steer decoding without retraining. LLM / VLM (Video-LLM) Post-training: RLVR and policy optimization, agentic RL for stateful agents, multimodal alignment, and continual learning, across LLMs and Video-LLMs.

This has produced 5 first-author papers at NeurIPS 2025 / CVPR 2026 / ICML 2026 / EMNLP 2026 / NeurIPS 2026 and 5 first-author submissions under review at ICLR 2027 and Nature. I also own the distributed training stack end-to-end, training LLMs and VLMs on up to 32 H100s (4 nodes × 8) across Samsung SDS Cloud, NHN Cloud (B200), and AWS (H200). Researcher on two Korean national AI projects (NRF, IITP); reviewer for NeurIPS, ICLR, ICML, CVPR, ECCV, and ACL ARR.

Heat-GRPO — Heat-Kernel Policy Optimization for Multi-Rubric RL ICLR 2027 (under review)

  • Built a multi-rubric RL (RLAIF) training pipeline in verl (FSDP + async vLLM rollouts) with a served Qwen3-32B LLM-judge returning per-rubric YES/NO log-prob margins as rewards.
  • Implemented and trained GRPO, GDPO, GD2PO, and Heat-GRPO on 6 policies × 4 benchmarks × 3 seeds on H100s — 288 full RL runs.
  • Heat-GRPO achieved the best mean in 23/24 conditions (up to +25%), held up under 3 additional judges, and cut advantage-computation cost by ~39% vs. GDPO / GD2PO.

What Gets Deployed? — Deployment Accounting for Stateful Agents ICLR 2027 (under review)

  • Trained LLM agents with multi-turn RL in interactive environments (ALFWorld, WebShop) using verl-agent + Ray: Qwen2.5-7B, full-parameter FSDP on 4×H100, 16 sandboxed environment rollouts per task, 50-step horizons.
  • Implemented and compared 10 agentic RL credit-assignment variants and exploratory AppWorld runs.
  • Built deployment-accounting evaluation (commit / wrong-commit / abstain): trained policies reached 72.5% / 71.3% committed success on seen / unseen rooms vs. 62.9% / 60.5% for GRPO, with outcome selection adding 9–13 points.

Subliminal RL — Tracing a Trait from Emission to Absorption Nature (under review)

  • Decomposed trait transfer through reward signals into emission, transmission through a semantically empty carrier, and absorption.
  • Built prompt-level vs. weight-level trait loading, zero-propositional-content carriers, trait-ablated controls, disjoint readout items, and fixed decision rules.
  • Added an observer axis separating semantic readability from statistical distinguishability.

Revisiting Continual Learning: A Three-Interface Diagnosis of Stability and Plasticity NeurIPS 2026 — Main

  • Showed BWT and FWT are interface-dependent across sequential head, fresh probe, and classifier-free backbone diagnostics.
  • Found a backbone-level stability–plasticity trade-off negative in all 150 clustering configurations (mean r = −0.74) across 7 datasets, multiple backbones up to 8B, and 8+ IL methods.
  • Introduced Just LM-Head Tuning (JLT), recovering +53 to +64 accuracy points on frozen incrementally trained backbones.

CLAWS — Creativity Detection for LLM-Generated Solutions NeurIPS 2025 — Main

  • Proposed a white-box detector using attention across prompt sections vs. output to classify solutions as Typical / Creative / Hallucinated.
  • Validated on five 7–8B math RL models over 4,545 problems from 181 contests, outperforming five white-box baselines.

Thinking Diffusion — Visual-Grounded Reasoning for Diffusion MLLMs CVPR 2026 — Main

  • Conducted the first quantitative analysis of CoT reasoning in dMLLMs, identifying Early Answer Generation and weak early-step visual grounding.
  • Proposed training-free Position & Step Penalty (PSP) and Visual Reasoning Guidance (VRG), yielding up to +7.5% accuracy and >3× faster inference vs. baselines using 4× more diffusion steps.

ST-Veto — Spatio-Temporal Token Veto for Diffusion MLLMs ICML 2026 — Main

  • Designed a training-free decoding-time veto-and-swap mechanism using confidence dynamics and visual grounding.
  • Achieved up to +9% accuracy over baseline decoding and prior VLM reasoning methods with zero extra training or generation cost.

Projector-JEPA — Two-Expert Residual Projector for Video-LLMs ICLR 2027 (under review)

  • Proposed a two-expert residual projector (Local Expert + State Expert, both Gated DeltaNet) that models inter-frame motion and world dynamics.
  • Introduced JEPA-style auxiliary objectives and trained an ~80K-parameter module for one epoch on 70K public samples, yielding up to ~7% gains across 7 Video-LLMs on 5 benchmarks.
